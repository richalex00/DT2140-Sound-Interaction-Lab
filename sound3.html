    • Pasta carbonara

    Recipe reader: tilt the phone side to side

    Use case: your hands are covered in flour and you need the next step of the recipe without touching the screen.

    Mapping: tilt the phone to the right (about 25°) to go to the next step, to the left to go back; bring it back level before the next turn. Every step plays a marimba note, and the note goes up as you advance, so its pitch tells you how far you are in the recipe. The last step plays a short arpeggio.

    Why: tilting is a coarse gesture you can do with your wrist, like turning a page, and the rising note confirms the action and your position without looking at the screen.
