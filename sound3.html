    • Mapping 3 — Raise the Baton, the Brass Swells (Musical Conductor)

    Action: the phone is used to point straight up. Sound: brass. The higher the phone is raised, the louder the brass plays. Pointing straight up gives full volume; lying flat is silent.

    Why: conductors raise their arms to ask for a crescendo and to lead into a climax, and up commonly means more (louder, higher, stronger). The angle of the phone sets the brass's blowing pressure, so the sound swells continuously as the baton rises and fades as it comes down. It is a continuous mapping rather than a trigger, so the conductor shapes the dynamics in real time, as with a real brass section.
