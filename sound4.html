    Kneading coach: only very large movements

    Use case: recipes say "knead for 10 minutes", but what counts is the work you put in, not the clock. Put the phone in your apron pocket (or strap it to your forearm) and knead or whisk.

    Mapping: only large movements count, small ones are ignored. You become the stand mixer: a motor sound turns on and follows your pace, faster when you work harder, and it rises slowly as the dough develops. When you have done enough work (about 8 s of full effort in this demo) the motor spins down and stops: the dough is ready.

    Why: the motor of a stand mixer is the familiar sound of kneading, so the sound tells you that your effort is being counted and when you can stop.
