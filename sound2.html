    Hourglass (egg / tea timer): stand the phone up

    Use case: a quick timer for boiled eggs or tea, with nothing to set. Stand the phone upright on the counter like an hourglass.

    Mapping: while the phone stands upright you hear the sand running (15 s in this demo, 3 minutes for eggs). In the last part the stream gradually gets thinner and quieter, so you can hear that time is running out before it is over; then it stops. As with a real hourglass, laying the phone down pauses the sand, and turning it upside down makes the sand run back, so the time that has passed comes back.

    Why: the sound is the hourglass itself, so you know the timer is running and when it is almost over without looking. The sand is our own Faust model built on the rain model (faust-dsp/Kitchen/hourglass.dsp).
