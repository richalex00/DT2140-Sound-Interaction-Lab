    Kitchen timer: spin the phone flat on the counter

    Use case: you are cooking with dirty hands and cannot touch the screen. Lay the phone on the counter and turn it like the knob of a mechanical kitchen timer (you can even push it with the back of your hand).

    Mapping: every 30° clockwise (one "hour" on a clock face) adds one step (10 s in this demo, 1 minute in real use) and you hear one "tick"; turning anticlockwise removes time. When you stop turning, the countdown starts with a soft tick every second. At zero a small bell rings. Turning the phone again stops the bell.

    Why: the sound copies the real object, a ratchet click for every step and a bell at the end, so you can set and follow the timer without looking. The sound is our own Faust model (faust-dsp/Kitchen/kitchenTimer.dsp).
