    • Mapping 1 — The Downbeat (Musical Conductor)

    Action: the phone is held like a conductor's baton and detects very large movements, but not small ones. Sound: a djembe drum beat. A harder swing gives a louder beat.

    Why: we imagined the phone as a conductor's baton. A conductor marks the beat with a big, deliberate downward swing, so only large movements play a beat and small hand tremors stay silent. The strength of the swing sets the loudness, just as a bigger gesture from a conductor asks the orchestra to play louder. A short pause after each beat makes one swing equal one beat.
